Online Webinar 6:
AI in Cybersecurity Analytics

NARRATIVE REPORT

On September 10, 2026, I attended a two-hour online webinar called “AI in Cybersecurity Data Analytics in the 21st Century: New Innovation of Technology.” The webinar was organized by Ethel Programming Computer Programming Services and was conducted through Google Meet. It was an interesting and informative experience that allowed me to learn more about how artificial intelligence, data, and cybersecurity are becoming increasingly connected in today’s digital world.

One of the topics that caught my attention was AI bias and data quality. The speaker explained how poor-quality data can lead to poor insights and eventually poor decisions. We discussed different problems that can affect data, such as bias, incomplete information, incorrect data, outdated data, and data that does not properly represent the people or situations being studied. This made me realize that AI is not simply about having advanced technology; the quality and reliability of the data behind it are equally important.

Another part of the webinar focused on the relationship between data, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity. The presentation showed how these three areas work together to help create trusted digital systems. I learned that AI can be useful in identifying patterns, analyzing information, and supporting cybersecurity efforts, but it also needs to be used carefully and responsibly.

The webinar also discussed the idea of moving from automation to intelligence. Automation allows a system to perform a task automatically, while intelligence allows a system to understand what is happening and respond appropriately. The presentation then introduced the concept of adaptive systems, which can adjust their actions based on changing conditions. I found this particularly interesting because it showed how technology is gradually becoming more capable of responding to real-world situations instead of simply following fixed instructions.

Overall, attending the webinar was a valuable learning experience for me. It gave me a better understanding of how AI, data analytics, and cybersecurity are connected and why data quality is so important when developing intelligent systems. I also gained a greater appreciation for the role of responsible technology in creating secure and trustworthy digital environments. I am grateful for the opportunity to participate in the webinar and expand my knowledge about technologies that are becoming increasingly important in the modern world.
