Online Webinar 5:
ESP8266: Power-Up – Turning Ideas Into Smart Projects

NARRATIVE REPORT

On September 9, 2026, I attended a one-hour online webinar called “ESP8266: Power-Up – Turning Ideas Into Smart Projects” through Google Meet. The webinar was organized by Ethel Programming Computer Programming Services.

During the webinar, I learned more about the ESP8266 and how it can be used to create smart and connected projects. The discussion helped me understand how small devices like the ESP8266 can connect to the internet and be used to control or monitor different things.

I found the topic interesting because it showed me how programming and technology can be combined to turn simple ideas into working projects. It also gave me a better understanding of how smart devices and Internet of Things (IoT) projects are developed.

Overall, I enjoyed attending the webinar because I learned something new that I can use in my studies and future projects. It also encouraged me to explore more about electronics, programming, and IoT. The experience was helpful and gave me new ideas about what I can create using technology.
