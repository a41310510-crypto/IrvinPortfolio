Online Webinar 3:
Tech Revolution

NARRATIVE REPORT

On September 13, 2026, I attended a one-hour online webinar called “Tech Revolution: Transforming the World Through Innovation” through Google Meet. The webinar was organized by Ethel Programming Computer Programming Services.

During the webinar, I learned how technology continues to change the way we live, study, work, and communicate. I learned about technology, innovation, digital transformation, and how new ideas can make our daily lives easier. The discussion also helped me understand how technology can affect different industries and society.

One of the topics that caught my attention was Artificial Intelligence (AI). I learned about generative AI, machine learning, computer vision, AI assistants, and automation. We also discussed other technologies such as cloud computing, the Internet of Things, robotics, big data, 5G, cybersecurity, blockchain, and extended reality.

Overall, I enjoyed attending the webinar because I learned many new things about modern technology. It made me realize how quickly technology is developing and how important it is to keep learning about it. As a student, I can use the knowledge I gained from this webinar in my studies and future career. It was a helpful and interesting experience that made me more curious about technology and innovation.
