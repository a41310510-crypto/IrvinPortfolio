Online Webinar 7:
Fundamentals of Database Management

NARRATIVE REPORT

On September 2, 2026, I attended a two-hour webinar entitled “Fundamentals of Database Management”, conducted by the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) Region V – Catanduanes Provincial Office through Google Meet.

The webinar gave me an opportunity to learn more about the basic concepts and importance of database management. I learned how databases help organize, store, manage, and retrieve information efficiently. As an Information Technology student, I found the topic relevant to my studies because databases are an important part of many systems and applications that we develop.

During the webinar, I gained a better understanding of the importance of proper database management in keeping information organized and accessible. I also realized that having knowledge of databases is useful not only for academic projects but also for my future career in the IT field.

Overall, attending the webinar was a valuable learning experience for me. It helped me gain additional knowledge about database management and encouraged me to continue improving my technical skills. The experience also gave me a better appreciation of how databases contribute to the development of reliable and efficient information systems.
