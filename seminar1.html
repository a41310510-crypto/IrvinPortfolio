Online Webinar 1:
Data Securrity 101

NARRATIVE REPORT

On September 26, 2026, I attended a two-hour online webinar entitled “Data Security 101: Protect, Prevent and Response” through Google Meet. The webinar was organized by Ethel Programming Computer Programming Services.

During the webinar, I learned about the importance of keeping our personal information and data safe. I realized that we need to be careful when using the internet, computers, and different online applications because our information can be at risk if we are not careful. I also learned the importance of preventing security threats and knowing what to do when a security problem occurs.

As a student, this webinar helped me understand more about data security and how I can apply it in my daily life. It reminded me to protect my personal information, use technology responsibly, and be more aware of possible online threats. Overall, attending this webinar was a good learning experience for me. I gained new knowledge that I can use in my studies and in the future. I am thankful for the opportunity to attend and learn more about the importance of protecting our data and staying safe online.
